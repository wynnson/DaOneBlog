I Am Wrong


I heard this saying (by AI) that we are so concerned about machines being human that we forget that humans have become machines. We ask it what it means to be human like it has the answer. Maybe we weren't meant to do this. If it was automatable it wasn't a problem in the first place. So I believe there must be something else we will do or are meant to do. Incalculable. I admit, I am wrong.
